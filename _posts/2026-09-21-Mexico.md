## Introduction 
It's always been Mikaela's dream (especially after covid curtailed her previous plans) to go to Mexico, and we finally went in April, spending the month there, partly to celebrate her 30th birthday. We both absolutely loved it, the food, the culture, the people, and the place were just amazing. 

## Vancouver
We flew with Air Canada (who were very good), with our stopover in Vancouver. It was only an hour more than going via LA or SF, and also a bit longer than flying via Dallas. We had 12 hours on the way there. The weather was very grim, but we still enjoyed it.

![gastown](https://i.imgur.com/Mb77jTJ.jpeg)
First stop was Gas Town as we walked around looking for a Tim Hortons (not hard to find). The old town was really pretty.

![Granville Island](https://i.imgur.com/mAQL7K2.jpeg)
We then walked over to Granville Island, we really liked it and the market there. Had some great seafood for lunch and a nice beer. From there it was back to the airport (which felt like the 90s) to get our connecting flight to Mexico City.

## CDMX

![cmdxdinner](https://i.imgur.com/nqI7cvz.jpeg)
After about 36 hours of travel, we finally were in our AirBNB in the neighborhood of Roma at about 2AM. There was a small taqueria practically next door open, and we had a quick bite to eat, it was great.


![cdmxcathederal](https://i.imgur.com/zoSiN33.jpeg)
On our first full day we headed to the centre of Mexico City to visit the cathedral and the main square. 


![templemayor](https://i.imgur.com/l0EilQR.jpeg)
Next to it, are the old Aztec ruins of Temple Mayor



![chapultepec](https://i.imgur.com/wcdamBp.jpeg)
We then went to Chapultepec park, which was super busy as it was the holidays, and to the castle overlooking it, which was stunning.


![engagement](https://i.imgur.com/mhfYjA2.jpeg)

![luchalibre](https://i.imgur.com/ubiCLkg.jpeg)

### Teotihuacan
We took a day trip out to visit the two pyramids just outside Mexico City called Teotihuacan.

![teotihuacan](https://i.imgur.com/SmmX6EJ.jpeg)
It was quite warm, and we had to queue for a while to get in, but definitely worth it. I also climbed one of them, which was serious work.

![lagruta](https://i.imgur.com/VlF0VUK.jpeg)
We then had lunch in this restaurant, La Gruta, which is inside a candlelit cave. It was one of the best meals I had on the trip, and the atmosphere was amazing.

## Puebla
After about 4 days in Mexico City we caught a bus out to the old town of Puebla for a few nights.

![puebla](https://i.imgur.com/jhHsIHb.jpeg)
The view from our hotel was great, with an amazing balcony.

![Mural de los Poblanos](https://i.imgur.com/BymNRl2.jpeg) 

![Mural de los Poblanos](https://i.imgur.com/qji5UAh.jpeg)

We had an amazing dinner, where we both had mole, at Mural de los Poblanos, to celebrate our engagement. The restaurant, named from the mural, was stunning.

## Puerto Escondido

![puertoescondido](https://i.imgur.com/eiTuu4t.jpeg)
After a short bus back to CMDX, we caught a flight down to the pacific coast town of Puerto Escondido. It was really quite hot, but we loved it.  

![puertoescondido](https://i.imgur.com/MwHV1Ho.jpeg)
The sunset was really, really pretty here. Everyone flocks to the beach to watch it. 

We stayed in a really nice hotel with a great pool, so we spent one day at the beach, and one day at the pool, which was just as nice. I also enjoyed watching UCL live by a bar on the beach. Watching games live at reasonable times was fantastic.

## Oaxaca
